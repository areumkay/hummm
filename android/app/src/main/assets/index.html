Pick a frequency

Each ear gets a slightly different tone. Your brain hears the difference as a slow pulse, the beat. Tap a row to set it.

Controls

Frequency is the beat: the gap between the left and right tones.

Tone is the pitch both tones sit around. Lower is softer to listen to; 100–250 Hz suits most people.

Volume sets the beats only. Each noise layer has its own level.

Binaural sends one tone to each ear and needs headphones. Switch to monaural to play both tones in both ears, so the pulse works through a speaker too.

Sleep timer fades everything out over the last 30 seconds, then stops.

Notes

Works offline once loaded and stores nothing but your settings on this device. Effects of binaural beats vary from person to person and the research is mixed. Keep the volume low, and don't listen while driving.
